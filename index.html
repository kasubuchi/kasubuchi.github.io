RESEARCHER PROFILE

Kiyotaka Kasubuchi

粕渕 清孝

Principal, Pionira Solutions LLC — Uji, Kyoto, Japan

kasubuchi [at] gmail.com

Research interests

  • Hallucination in large language models
  • Frequency structure and geometry of meaning in language-model representations
  • Neuro-symbolic AI: grounding terms and concepts in explicit ontologies
  • Retrieval-augmented generation for manufacturing knowledge
  • Quality of software and of system development documents

Background

Trained in mathematics and software engineering, with a long career in software efficiency and software quality: building the mechanisms that keep a system demonstrably correct while its performance improves.

Later served as an external technical advisor at a large manufacturing company, contributing to the direction of AI adoption and to technology selection within its R&D organisation, on themes including practical deployment of generative AI, internal knowledge retrieval, and the digitalisation of skills transfer.

Founded Pionira Solutions LLC in 2024. Works principally with manufacturers and systems integrators, combining advisory work on AI adoption with in-house research.
